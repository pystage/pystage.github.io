There is not much yet, and it will grow. What you can use today:

- [Projects](/projects/): step-by-step projects for students. The first one is [Make the zombie walk and talk](/projects/walk-and-talk/).
- [Start](/start/): installing Python and PyStage and the first program, written for students.
- [Convert](/convert/): students turn their own Scratch projects into Python and compare.
- [Documentation](https://pystage.readthedocs.io/) on Read the Docs: the technical reference (English).
- [Online converter](https://convert.pystage.org): no installation needed on the teacher side.

Planned: lesson plans, worksheets and a comparison of Scratch concepts and Python concepts. If you have ideas or want to contribute material, tell us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/MSGrs3UCCz) or in the [issue tracker](https://github.com/pystage/pystage/issues).
